Where fireplace ad budgets quietly leak

The three gaps we see again and again in home-service accounts, and the fix that goes in from day one.
Leak 01

Phone calls go untracked

Homeowners call before they book. If those calls are not counted as conversions, Google optimizes toward form fills only and the true cost per lead stays invisible.

Fix: call conversion tracking, every call tied to the campaign and keyword that drove it.
Leak 02

The homepage is the landing page

Ad clicks land on a busy homepage with no single offer and no clear call button, so paid traffic bounces and the click is wasted.

Fix: a dedicated page, one offer, mobile-first, form and click-to-call above the fold.
Leak 03

No negative keywords

Budget burns on renters, DIY, repair-part, and job-seeker searches that never buy, pushing the cost per real lead up fast.

Fix: an airtight negative list from launch, refined weekly on the search-terms report.
